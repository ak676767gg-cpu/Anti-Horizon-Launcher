# Security

## Reporting a problem

If you find a security problem in the Anti-Horizon Client (anything that could harm players, their accounts, their
computers or their worlds), please report it **privately**, not in a public issue:

- Use **[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/ak676767gg-cpu/Anti-Horizon-Launcher/security/advisories/new)** on this repository's Security tab, or
- the contact page at <https://anti-horizon.com/play/contact.html>.

Say what you found, which versions it affects, and how to reproduce it if you can. You'll get a reply within a few days,
and a fix as soon as possible. Once it's fixed and released, you're welcome to be credited.

## Fake copies

The only official places to get the client are the repository's [Releases](https://github.com/ak676767gg-cpu/Anti-Horizon-Launcher/releases), the Anti-Horizon
Launcher, and anti-horizon.com. Each release lists the SHA-256 fingerprint of its jar so you can check your file.
Copies from anywhere else may have been changed, so please report them through the contact page above.

## What the client can access

See [Why you can trust it](README.md#why-you-can-trust-it) in the README: it never touches your login or tokens, makes
one download (the menu background, fingerprint-checked) and sends nothing.
