A look inside

Everything happens in one window over a black hole: your instances, your mods, your skin and the game itself.

  • Your instances

    Each has its own Minecraft version, mod loader, worlds and mods. Hover one and press Play; the logs open with it.

  • Everything in one place

    Click an instance for its mods, resource packs, shaders and data packs, its files and its worlds. Share it with a friend as one file.

  • Skins

    See your skin before you wear it. Pick one of Minecraft's default skins, one from your own skin packs, or upload your own.

  • Mods in one click

    Search Modrinth by category and sort by downloads, follows or updates. Everything a mod needs comes with it, so the game starts every time.

  • A page for every mod

    Screenshots, the full description, its links, and whether it's needed on your side or the server's, without leaving the launcher.

  • Kept up to date

    Every mod by its real name and version. Switch them on and off, and update them all at once. A stable mod only ever gets stable updates.

  • Eleven black holes

    One on each page, one everywhere, or a new mix each time it opens.

  • Updates itself

    New versions download in the background. One click on Update installs them, and the launcher is back in a moment.

Made to be trusted

A launcher signs in to your Microsoft account, so you should know exactly what it does. Here's all of it.

  • Your password stays with Microsoft

    You sign in on Microsoft's own page with a short code. The launcher never sees your password, and the sign-in it keeps can only be read by you: on Windows it's encrypted for your user.

  • Nothing is tracked

    No analytics, no telemetry, no ads. It talks only to Microsoft, Mojang, the mod loaders, Modrinth, this site for updates and our cosmetics service.

  • Signed updates

    Every update is signed, and the launcher refuses one that isn't, so nobody can slip it a fake.

  • Read the code

    The launcher and the client are on GitHub for anyone to read and check. Nothing is hidden or scrambled, and GitHub builds the Mac and Linux versions from it.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The launcher is free, with no ads and nothing to buy. You need your own copy of Minecraft: Java Edition to play.

Is my Microsoft account safe?

You sign in on Microsoft's own page with a short code, so the launcher never sees your password. The sign-in it keeps can only be read by your own user on your computer (on Windows it's encrypted), and you can sign out any time. The code that does it is public on GitHub.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Windows warns about new apps it hasn't seen many people install yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. You only see it once: after that, the launcher updates itself without it.

Will it change my .minecraft folder?

No. The launcher keeps its instances, worlds and mods in a folder of its own, so your normal Minecraft setup stays exactly as it is.

Which versions and mod loaders does it support?

Every Minecraft: Java Edition version, from the newest release back to the first alphas, with Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge.

What's the Anti-Horizon client?

A small Fabric mod that comes with the launcher: a Right Shift menu, twelve HUD pieces (FPS, CPS, combo, reach, armour and more), quick switches for your mods, and an aurora behind the title screen. It goes into Fabric instances on 1.21.11 and 26.1 to 26.3, and you can turn that off in Settings.

What are cosmetics?

Capes made from our black holes, gold or void wings, a halo, an accretion ring or a crown. Pick them in the launcher's Cosmetics tab and everyone who plays with the Anti-Horizon client sees them on you, on any server. They're free, and they only change how you look.

Can I get mods from CurseForge?

Not yet. Mods, resource packs, shaders and modpacks come from Modrinth, which has most of the same mods. CurseForge is coming in an update.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Yes. There's a Mac version for Apple silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 10.15 or newer), and a Linux version as an AppImage, a .deb and an .rpm. They do everything the Windows version does, and update themselves the same way. Download them here.

Why does my Mac say it can't check the app?

Apple checks apps from developers who pay for its programme; this one is free and made by one person. Open it once, then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. You only do this once: updates open straight away.

Is it an official Minecraft launcher?

No. It's made by an independent developer. Not an official Minecraft product. Not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.
