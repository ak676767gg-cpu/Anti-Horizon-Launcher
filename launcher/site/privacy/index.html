Privacy

The short version: we don't collect anything about you. There are no accounts with us, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, in the launcher, the client or on this website.

The launcher

  • Signing in. You sign in on Microsoft's own page with a short code. The launcher never sees your password.
  • Your sign-in afterwards. The tokens Microsoft and Minecraft give back stay on your computer: on Windows they're encrypted for your Windows user, and on a Mac or Linux they're in a file only your user can open. They go only to Microsoft, Xbox and Minecraft's sign-in services, and to Minecraft when it starts.
  • Who it talks to. Microsoft, Xbox and Minecraft services (sign-in and your skin), Mojang (the game and its files), Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge (mod loaders), Modrinth and CurseForge (mods), this website (launcher updates), on a Mac or Linux GitHub (where those updates are kept), and Anti-Horizon's cosmetics service. Those services have their own privacy policies.
  • Cosmetics. When you pick capes, wings or head pieces, the launcher signs you in to Anti-Horizon's cosmetics service the way a Minecraft server checks a player: Mojang confirms the account is yours, so the service never sees your password or your Minecraft sign-in. It keeps your Minecraft player ID, the cosmetics you picked and when you last changed them, so other players can see them. Choose None for all three and that record is deleted.
  • Your files. Instances, worlds, mods, skin packs and settings stay in the launcher's own folder on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, except a skin you choose to save to your Minecraft account.

The Anti-Horizon client

The in-game mod downloads one file from this website, the background behind the title screen, once. To show cosmetics, it asks Anti-Horizon's cosmetics service which ones the players around you wear, sending only those players' IDs: nothing about you, your world, your chat or the server you're on. Switch Cosmetics off in its menu and it stops asking. It reads your username only to show it in its menu.

This website

No cookies, no analytics and no ads. It's hosted on Cloudflare, which, like any web host, handles the network requests your browser makes (such as your IP address) to deliver pages and keep the site safe. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Children

We don't collect information from anyone, of any age. Minecraft accounts are Microsoft's, under Microsoft's rules.

Changes and questions

If this ever changes, this page will say so first, with a new date at the top. Questions: see Contact. You can also check all of the above yourself: the code is public.
